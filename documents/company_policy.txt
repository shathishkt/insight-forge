InsightForge Demo Company Policy

Product Returns

Customers may return products within 30 days
of purchase if the product is unused and in
its original packaging.

Products that have been damaged by the customer
are not eligible for a standard return.

Refunds are normally processed within seven
business days after the returned product has
been inspected.

Premium customers may receive priority handling
for eligible returns.

Customer Support

Customer support is available Monday through
Friday.

Priority customers receive faster response
times.

Product Strategy

The company considers laptops a strategic
growth category because of their higher average
profit contribution.

Management should monitor laptop sales,
profitability, and regional demand when making
future investment decisions.
